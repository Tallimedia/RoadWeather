**Full description**

```
Finland's own road and weather data, in the car.

FIRoadWeather shows what Finnish instruments are reporting right now — road surface
temperature from the Finnish Meteorological Institute's road stations, and road surface
condition from Fintraffic's sensor network. Not a global model's guess at Finland.

ROAD SURFACE
Surface temperature from the nearest road weather station, with the station named and its
distance shown, so you know where the reading came from. Road condition — dry, moist, wet
or worse — from Fintraffic's own sensors, plus a road condition outlook for the hours
ahead on your road section.

ICE RISK
A frost and ice warning derived from the numbers that decide it: surface temperature
against freezing, and surface temperature against the dew point. Air temperature above
zero does not mean the road is.

WARNINGS
Nearby road notices from Fintraffic — lane closures, roadworks, speed restrictions —
sorted by distance, plus official weather warnings from the Finnish Meteorological
Institute. Only what is relevant to where you are.

WEATHER
Current conditions and the forecast for where you are, from the Finnish Meteorological
Institute's own post-processed forecast — the same one behind ilmatieteenlaitos.fi, not a
global model interpolated over Finland. Forecast hours shown with at-a-glance icons.

BUILT FOR THE CAR
Driving-optimised screens built with Android's car app templates. Readable at a glance,
and nothing that asks for attention it should not have.

PRIVACY
No account, no sign-in, no tracking. The app does not store anything about you.

DATA SOURCES
Weather and road station observations from the Finnish Meteorological Institute,
licensed CC BY 4.0.
Road conditions and road condition forecasts: Source: Fintraffic / digitraffic.fi,
license CC 4.0 BY.
This app is independent and is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, either organisation.

Coverage is Finland only.

This app is for general information. It is not a substitute for your own judgement, for
official warnings, or for driving to the conditions.
```

## Notes

- **Attribution is not optional.** FMI's CC BY 4.0 and Fintraffic's CC 4.0 BY both require
  it, and it has to appear **in the app** as well as in the listing.
- **The "not affiliated" line is deliberate.** Naming FMI and Fintraffic is required by the
  licences; implying endorsement is a trademark problem. The disclaimer separates the two.
- **The closing disclaimer matters more than usual.** This app tells people whether a road
  is about to freeze. `www.tallimedia.com/terms` carries the same position.
- Do not add features to this copy before they ship. A listing describing a warnings
  screen that does not exist is a review rejection, not a rounding error.
